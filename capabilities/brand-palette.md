# Rookworks working brand palette

Sampled from the supplied Rookworks avatar:
- Primary indigo: **#472F7A**
- Charcoal grey: **#231F20**

Proposed split-complementary accents (indigo hue ≈259°; accents ≈49° and ≈109°):
- Muted gold: **#DEC66B** — impact figures and small process markers.
- Sage green: **#A2C995** — supporting process accents.

Supporting interface colors:
- Light indigo: **#C1A7F0** — readable links and labels on dark surfaces.
- Panel: **#2D2833**
- Primary text: **#F2EFF4**
- Secondary text: **#BBB4C3**
- Border: **#4B4257**

Use the original indigo for primary actions and prominent surfaces, charcoal for the page foundation, and the two split complements sparingly. Light indigo supplies readable foreground accents without changing the core brand color.
